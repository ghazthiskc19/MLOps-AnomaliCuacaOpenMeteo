Warm-up decision: exclude raw positions 0..47 from fit/scoring, keeping them as
context. Splits are calculated after exclusion; counts differ from preliminary
EDA. Three folds use equal validation durations via TimeSeriesSplit. A class
missing from training rejects the evaluation; missing validation classes are
reported explicitly and never presented as measured recall.

No actuator deployment, remote publishing, dataset regeneration, broad LK04
refactor, or silent correction of corrupt sensor values is included.
